a two-tenant cross-test covers disks, tokens, /system and quota; the mirror pair is wired only by `Config.peers` (the uni-config, operator#17): `BLOBSTORE_PEERS` is no longer read (#10); every record-plane request an RPC causes (each page and the close of a list included) carries the RPC's `trace_id`, tested per handler over the generated client, and the sweep, the stats flush and the anti-entropy pass are marked `// trace: unrelated:` (#11); DeleteBlob's peer `DELETE /peer/blob/…` carries the RPC's `trace_id` in `X-Trace-Id` and the peer logs the unlink under it, tested on the two-pod harness (#12); `security-exceptions.yaml` accepts gosec G703 (path traversal, a taint false positive on `store.go`'s temp and blob paths) until 2027-02-07 (#15)
